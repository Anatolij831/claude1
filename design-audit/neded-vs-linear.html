Design audit · neded.de (LUMA·SKIN) vs linear.app · October 2026

Your page uses everything. Linear uses almost nothing.

Linear looks expensive because it commits: one typeface, three weights, one accent, one dark canvas, and hairline borders instead of shadows. Your shop uses 41 colours, two typefaces, six weights and changes background ten times. Every element asks for attention, so none of them gets it.

The gaps

Seven differences, by visual impact

Each row shows your current style next to the target: Linear's rules applied to LUMA.

One canvas

Linear never changes the background. Sections are separated by 80–128px of darkness, with no fills and no dividers. Your page switches white → beige → black → white → beige → white → black → white → red gradient → black, so the eye restarts at every band.

Your product emits light. On a near-black canvas the LED glow becomes the only colour on the page, which is exactly the job Linear gives its indigo.

Fixbody{background:#08090a} section{background:none; padding-block:128px}

One accent

Linear runs on greys: four for text, three for surfaces, plus one indigo that only appears on things you can click. Your code has 41 colours: gold, beige, orange, pink, PayPal yellow, star amber and eight wavelength colours used as decoration on cards and badges.

Keep greys and one red. Red appears only where the mask's light appears: the glow and the active light mode.

Fix--light:#ff3b3b /* glow + active mode only */ delete: --gold --bg-soft --paypal #f5a623 #b8540a #ffe9e9

One typeface, three weights, set tight

Linear sets everything in Inter: 400 to read, 510 for UI, 590 for emphasis, never bold. Headlines run 48–72px with −0.022em tracking, so they feel engineered. You pair Fraunces with Inter, use six weights up to 800, track headlines at −0.01em and stop at 35px.

The serif brings boutique-cosmetics warmth. That pulls against the clinical precision you like in Linear.

Fixfont-family:Inter; font-weight:510; letter-spacing:-.022em; font-feature-settings:"cv01","ss03" /* drop Fraunces, drop 700/800 */

Borders instead of shadows

Linear builds depth with 1px borders at 8% white and fills that step from 2% to 5% white. Buttons get 6px corners; pills are only for tags. You use 8 radius values, pill buttons, 50px-blur drop shadows, coloured glows under badges and a 5px hover lift on cards.

Fixborder:1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.08); box-shadow:none; radius 6 (buttons) · 8 (cards) · 12 (panels); hover = brighter fill, no lift

Say it once, quietly

Linear's first screen holds a headline, one line of subtext and two buttons. Yours holds 13 competing elements. The discount styling (red price, strikethrough, pink “Spare” pill, orange ⚡ box, gold PayPal) signals a cheap gadget, which is the opposite of the premium device you're pricing at €99.

Fix.price-now{color:#f7f8f8;font-weight:590} .price-old{color:#62666d} remove .badge-sale .price-save .stock-note .announce; use PayPal's black button

Precision in the content

Linear's brand is precision, so contradictions hurt more in this style. Your page gives different numbers for the same thing, and a careful buyer will notice.

Order of work

Biggest change for the least effort first

  1. Swap tokens and fonts. One dark canvas, Inter only, weights 400/510/590. This alone moves the page most of the way.
  2. Strip the buy box. Remove the marquee, badge, pink pill, urgency box and emoji row. Restyle the price and use PayPal's black button.
  3. Trade shadows for borders. 1px at 8% white, radii 6/8/12, no hover lift.
  4. Fix the numbers. One wavelength table and one proof figure across the whole page.
  5. Reshoot on black. Product images where the LED glow is the only colour.
  6. Rebuild the phone fold. Headline and price above a 4:3 image.
